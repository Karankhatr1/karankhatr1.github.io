THE SHORT VERSION

Rupeezy had two apps: a trading app carrying 97% of company revenue, and a separate mutual fund app.

A SEBI regulation cut trading revenue by roughly 75%, and merging stopped being optional. The obvious answer was a mode switch between products, so that's what shipped first.

A switch holds two products, and commodities were already coming. I designed the model that replaced it: Navigation split into two axes, one for the journey and one for the product.

Architectural choices and trade-offs

Only lived on Home screen. Users deep in stock pages were stranded.
CHOSEN
Bottom navigation
Present on every screen. Context switch is always one tap away.
