Opportunities

4 Areas
  • Skating Power and Efficiency — Focus on off-ice and on-ice training to deepen his knee bend. A lower stance will translate to a more powerful and efficient stride, increasing his top-end speed and stability during physical contact.
  • Backhand Skill Development — Dedicate practice time to improving puck control, passing, and shooting on the backhand. Increased confidence on his weaker side will make him a more versatile and unpredictable offensive player.
  • Shot Diversification — Work on developing a quick and accurate one-timer. This will make him a greater threat without the puck in the offensive zone, especially on the power play, as he already has the intelligence to find open space.
  • Puck Handling Vision — Continue drills that focus on carrying the puck with his head up. This will allow him to better utilize his high-level vision and anticipation skills to identify passing and shooting lanes earlier.
