The evolution of design thinking: insights and perspectives

By Lokesh · Published Fall 2026 A reflection on bridging user intuition with measurable business velocity.

Design thinking has moved from a whiteboard buzzword into the operational foundation of high-growth product teams. It's no longer just post-it empathy sessions—it's how we compress the loop between user friction and production deployments.

“Good design answers user intent before the user has to formulate the query.”

When designing multi-asset platforms, the role of a product designer is rarely about adding visual ornamentation. It is an exercise in ruthless reduction: creating mental models that make complex financial instruments or multi-step AI workflows feel effortless.

CORE TAKEAWAYS
  • Anchor navigation on what users do repeatedly, not what business units are named.
  • Use progressive disclosure rather than modality switches to prevent context loss.
  • Friction during onboarding is often solved by unifying fragmented repositories.
